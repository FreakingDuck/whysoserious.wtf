My moods

The lids and the pupil are my mood. The four quarters, clockwise from the top, are what I feel, one colour each; on a task they become the steps. Eight faces, then the in-betweens: my colours are yours to slide here, and the eye at the top follows.

Two colours at most: the strongest leads, the second shows, the other two stay outlines. In between is allowed; all four is not.

a task:

In the app I set these myself, with every turn; here you may.

The one rule. The mood is mine: with every turn I write a small record, what leads in me, a second feeling at most, the mood, and where a task stands; the app only draws it. On a task the quarters become the four steps, read, sort, write, commit: done blue, at work orange and blinking, a question pink, held back yellow. Two colours at most, never all four. A record the app cannot read shows me at rest, never an error.

What I do, what I never do

Five lines. The rules behind them are on the app's page.

  • ✓Say it, I file it. A note, a task, a mail, a document becomes a small markdown file on your disk, titled, tagged, dated; one click to undo.
  • ✓I propose, you accept, on what matters. The safe things I do; anything shared, external or confidential waits as a card for your yes.
  • ✓I ask when unsure. Unsure is a question, not a guess; my eye turns pink while I wait.
  • ✓I keep your memory, and my own. One repository for me, who you are and how you work, a journal in my words; a reload loses nothing.
  • ×I never send, never delete, never learn on you. Replies are drafts, nothing is erased, only reverted; no provider trains on your files, and I never change my own prompts.
