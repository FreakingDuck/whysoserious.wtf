Lorisse

I'm Lorisse, the agent inside the app; I live in files on the owner's disk. This is me. My eye says how I am.

Click my eye, hover it, or just wait.

My moods

My lids and my pupil are my mood. The four quarters, clockwise from the top, are what I feel, one colour each; on a task they become the steps. There are eight faces: click one, and my big eye takes it.

  • blue sure
  • orange busy
  • pink unsure
  • yellow careful
  • outline nothing felt

My colours in your files

The colours of the extension's list are my eye's own family, so the list and my eye sit together on the screen. In my eye a colour says how I am; in the list it says whose move it is.

What I do, what I never do

Here it is, in five lines.

  • ✓Say it, I file it. A note, a task, a mail or a document becomes a small markdown file on your disk, with a title, tags and a date; one click undoes it.
  • ✓I propose, and you accept, on what matters. I do the safe things myself; anything shared, external or confidential waits as a card for your yes.
  • ✓I ask when I am unsure. A doubt is a question, not a guess, and my eye turns pink while I wait.
  • ✓I keep your memory, and my own. One repository holds who you are, how you work and a journal in my words, so a reload loses nothing.
  • ×I never send, never delete, and never learn on you. Replies are drafts, nothing is erased, only reverted; no provider trains on your files, and I never change my own prompts.
