Lorisse · for VS Code

A VS Code extension for a project you work on with Lorisse.

It keeps track of a project you do not read the code of: where it stands, what waits for your read, what the machine spent. You read the pages and mark them, in the same window as Claude Code.

It writes into a page only on your click, with an Undo. It runs nothing of the repository, and reaches no network.

Try it on a made-up project ↓

Install

Today the extension comes as a package file, handed over with the method's repository. One command installs it, then you restart VS Code:

code --install-extension lorisse-<version>.vsix

Once it is on the VS Code marketplace, it will be three clicks: open the Extensions view, search Lorisse, click Install.

Keep track of the project

The project page opens in a tab of the window. It is made by the extension's own copy of the method's scripts, never by a script of the repository.

the dashboard

Where things stand

The gate's last run, what waits for you, and the parts with their stage. A page it names opens in the reader.

the road and the parts

What is being worked on

The road is the work in order. The parts are the road looked at deeper: each one opened shows its three pages, its stage, your marks, and the steps of its walk.

the tokens

What the machine spent

Today, the last seven days and the last thirty, each split by model, counted from Claude Code's own records on this machine.

The dollars are the API's list prices, not your invoice, and the view says so.

The stages of a page

Every page of the method carries a line under its title: who wrote it, and your mark. The extension shows that state everywhere the page appears: a sign and a few words in the list, a chip in the page's header.

never read

Shows grey, with the minutes it takes to read.

Asks your first read.

read · 11-18

Shows blue, with the day of your read.

Asks nothing. It is yours to mark further.

ok · 11-18

Shows green: you agree with it.

Asks nothing, until its text changes.

to rework

Shows pink, "waits for Lorisse".

Asks Lorisse to change it as your notes say, and to answer each note under it.

changed since your mark

Shows orange. It opens on its changes, said in words.

Asks your read of what changed. Each change can be okayed.

answered

Shows "1 answer · your read": Lorisse answered a note of yours.

Asks your read of the answer: okay it, or reply.

How you mark. Three buttons on the page itself, in its header and again at its end: Read Ok To rework. A click writes the line under the title, and nothing else in the page. Undo puts it back; Next opens the next page that waits.

How you leave a note. Click a block and type. Your note is written into the page, under that block. Lorisse answers under it in a session, and the two show as one card beside the text, never inside it.

Try it

A made-up command-line unit converter: two parts with their three pages each, and its docs. Click a document on the left. Click a mark, a switch, an okay, or a block to leave a note. All of it stays in this page: nothing is saved, nothing is sent.

The last group, the method, holds a real page of the method: the contract of its install, whole, as the reader shows it.
